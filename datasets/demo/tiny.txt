Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Pip. Pip loved to fly high in the sky.
One day, Pip saw a big shiny star. The star was beautiful and bright.
"I want to touch the star," Pip said.
Pip flew higher and higher, but the star was too far.
Pip became tired, but he did not give up.
Suddenly, a friendly wind blew Pip all the way to the top of a tall tree.
From the tree, the star looked very close. Pip felt happy and sang a song.
The next morning, Pip woke up and saw the warm sun. The sun made Pip feel warm and safe.
Pip realized that some things are beautiful from far away, and that is okay.
From that day on, Pip flew happily every day.
