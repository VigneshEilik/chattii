Lily and Ben were best friends. They loved to play in the green garden.
In the garden, there was a big apple tree. The apples were red and sweet.
"Let's pick some apples!" Ben suggested.
Lily smiled and nodded. "Yes, let's make a sweet apple pie."
They gathered ten apples in a small basket.
Then, they saw a little rabbit hopping near the grass.
The rabbit was white and soft. It looked hungry.
Lily offered a piece of apple to the rabbit. The rabbit ate it happily.
"Look! The rabbit likes apples too," Ben laughed.
They went home to make the pie with their mom. The pie tasted delicious.
They had a wonderful day in the garden.
